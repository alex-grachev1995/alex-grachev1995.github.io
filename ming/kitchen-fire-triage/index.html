🔥 KITCHEN FIRE TRIAGE

Five fires. Nine things within reach. You have seconds.
Fire needs four things, and putting one out means taking one away: heat · fuel · oxygen · the chain reaction that keeps it feeding itself. After every choice you will be told which corner you attacked — or that you attacked none of them and made things considerably worse.
Two tries per fire. Some fires have more than one right answer. One of the nine options looks almost identical to a correct one and will nearly take your eyebrows off.
Solved first try: 0

The pattern: nearly everything that works on a kitchen fire removes oxygen or heat. Almost nothing you own removes the fuel — which is why "turn the heat off and cover it" beats every heroic option.

And the one that nearly killed you: water is denser than oil, so it sinks under the flames before it flashes to steam — expanding roughly 1700× underneath a pan of burning fat. One tablespoon becomes about 25 litres of steam in a fraction of a second, and it takes the burning oil with it, upwards, into the room.

Flour is not baking soda. Same box, same colour, opposite outcome: finely divided starch suspended in air is a combustible dust. Grain silos explode for this reason.
