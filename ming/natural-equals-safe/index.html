🧪 VERDICT ON A WORD

Every bin has items in it. That's the whole point. Nature invented arsenic, tobacco, asbestos and the deadliest toxin known to science. Laboratories invented insulin, aspirin and the vaccine that erased smallpox.

And the sharpest pair on the board: vitamin C from an orange and vitamin C from a factory are the identical molecule. Your cells cannot tell them apart. "Natural" and "synthetic" describe where a molecule was born \u2014 not what it does to you.

"Natural" is a marketing word, not a safety test. The only safety test is a safety test.
