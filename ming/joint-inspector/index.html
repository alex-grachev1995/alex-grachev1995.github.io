🕵️ JOINT INSPECTOR

Review the joint families, then inspect ten joints one by one.
📖 Review first — the three joint families:
🔒 FIBROUS — fixed
Bones joined by dense connective tissue. No movement.
Examples: skull sutures, teeth in their sockets.
🟡 CARTILAGINOUS — slightly movable
Bones joined by cartilage (a cushion). Small, limited movement.
Examples: spine vertebrae, rib cage.
🟢 SYNOVIAL — freely movable
A fluid-filled cavity between the bones. The big movers: hinge, ball-and-socket, pivot, saddle, condyloid.
Examples: shoulder, hip, knee, elbow…
🏃 And the movements they allow:
Flexion / extension — bend / straighten (elbow, knee) · Rotation — turn around an axis (neck) · Abduction / adduction — move away from / toward the body midline · Circumduction — circular cone-shaped movement (shoulder, hip).
