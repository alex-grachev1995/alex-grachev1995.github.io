MEET THE GRANDCHILD — THE MODERN SCIENTIFIC METHOD
ASKquestion→ HYPOTHESIZEtestable guess→ EXPERIMENTcontrolled test→ ANALYZEdata & stats→ PUBLISHpeer review
The family resemblance: OBSERVED ≈ ASK · RECORDED ≈ data collection · QUANTIFIED ≈ ANALYZE · CONSTRUCTED ≈ EXPERIMENT · DISSEMINATED ≈ PUBLISH. Same skeleton, fancier words.
Two real differences: (1) The modern method makes nature the judge — a controlled experiment decides between hypotheses, so being wrong is cheap and fast. The Renaissance version trusted the eye and the workshop; it could observe beautifully but couldn't always *test*. (2) Sharing stopped being optional. Today a discovery isn't real until it survives peer review — journals, replication, public data. For the Dream Team, SHARE was a personal choice… and the smallest, most-skipped skill on the whole Wall decided who changed the world.
