🏅 SPORTS SCIENCE

Newton's laws & kinetic energy — learn the plays, then run the quiz.
Five-minute warm-up — the four ideas every athlete (and physics student) needs:
🥅 Newton's First Law — Inertia
An object in motion stays in motion, an object at rest stays at rest — unless a force acts on it.
Example: a hockey puck gliding on ice keeps going until something stops it.
🎳 Newton's Second Law — F = ma
Force = Mass × Acceleration
More mass = more force needed for the same acceleration.
Example: a bowling ball needs more force to accelerate than a baseball.
⚾ Newton's Third Law — Action / Reaction
For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction.
Example: when you hit a ball with a bat, the bat also feels a force back from the ball.
🏌️ Kinetic Energy
The energy an object has because it's moving: KE = ½ × mass × velocity²
Velocity is squared — speed matters MORE than mass!
Example: a golf ball hit with a fast swing has high KE and goes far.
