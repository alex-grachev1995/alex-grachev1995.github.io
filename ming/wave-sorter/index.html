Eight waves walk in. Sort each one: what's the family, what's the shape?
Crash course first — three ideas:
What is a wave? A disturbance that transfers energy from place to place without transferring matter. Waves carry information and/or energy.
Two families. Mechanical waves need a medium — air, water, rope (sound, ocean waves). Electromagnetic (EM) waves need nothing — they travel through vacuum (light, radio, microwaves).
Two shapes. Transverse: the medium moves up-down (or side-to-side) while the wave travels forward — light, water surface, a plucked string. Longitudinal: the medium compresses and expands along the direction of travel — sound in air.
