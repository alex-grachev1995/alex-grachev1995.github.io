🍄 Decomposer or Not?

Nature's cleanup crew — but who's really ON it?
Here's the test: does it secrete enzymes to digest dead matter OUTSIDE its body? Then it's a DECOMPOSER (fungi, bacteria). Does it eat dead matter and digest it INSIDE? That's a DETRITIVORE (worms, maggots). Everything else is NEITHER — even if it hangs around dead things! First try = ⭐⭐⭐!
🍄 Decomposer
Digests dead matter externally with enzymes, then absorbs the food. Fungi and bacteria!
🪱 Detritivore
Eats dead matter and digests it internally. Worms, maggots, woodlice!
🙅 Neither
Herbivores, carnivores, scavengers — and plain old dead stuff itself!
