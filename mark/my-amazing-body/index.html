🫀 My Amazing Body

Your body is a living machine — do you know which part does what?
Your body is a machine made of 11 organ systems working together. Look at each picture and name the system! The heart pumps (circulatory), lungs breathe (respiratory), the brain commands (nervous), stomach blends food (digestive), bones frame you (skeletal), muscles move you (muscular), and skin wraps it all up (integumentary). First try = ⭐⭐⭐!
❤️ Circulatory
Heart + blood vessels — delivers oxygen and food to every cell.
🫁 Respiratory
Lungs — trades oxygen in, carbon dioxide out.
🧠 Nervous
Brain + nerves — the boss that sends and receives messages.
🍽️ Digestive
Stomach + intestines — blends food into nutrients.
🦴 Skeletal
Bones — the frame that holds you up and protects organs.
💪 Muscular
Muscles — the engines that pull on bones to move you.
🖐️ Skin
The wrapper — protection, touch, and keeping water in.
