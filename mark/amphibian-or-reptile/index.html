🦎 Amphibian or Reptile?

These feet look similar — but their owners are very different animals!
Amphibians (frogs, toads, salamanders) have soft, moist skin and often webbed feet with no claws — they need water nearby. Reptiles (lizards, turtles, snakes) have dry scaly skin and clawed toes. Look at each foot and decide! But watch out — one of these animals is a sneaky trickster... First try = ⭐⭐⭐!
🐸 Amphibian
Soft moist skin, webbed feet, no claws. Lives near water — 'amphibian' means 'double life' (water + land).
🦎 Reptile
Dry scaly skin and claws. Even turtles and snakes count — scales are the giveaway!
