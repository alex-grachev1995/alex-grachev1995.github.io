🦴 Fossil Time Capsules

Dead things usually get eaten and recycled... unless something goes WRONG. What saved these ones?
For a fossil to form, decomposers must miss the party — the dead organism has to be protected from rot. Look at each preserved treasure and choose HOW it was saved: quick burial, amber, tar, freezing, or petrification! First try = ⭐⭐⭐!
🏜️ Rapid burial
Sand or mud covers the remains fast — no oxygen, no scavengers.
🟠 Amber
Sticky tree resin traps small creatures and hardens over millions of years.
❄️ Extreme cold/dry
Freezing or total dryness stops microbes from decaying the body.
🪨 Petrification
Minerals in groundwater replace the original material — turn to stone!
